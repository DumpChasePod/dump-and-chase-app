Meet the Hosts

Born just a stone's throw from the Ohio River, Sam is a 2002 graduate of Beaver Local High School. He graduated from Youngstown College of Massotherapy in 2004 (which closed down a year later) and from the American Broadcasting School in 2012 (again, it closed down shortly after). He attended his first Phantoms game in March 2014. Two years later, he made the leap and became a season ticket holder, starting with the 2016–17 season.

Sam has played guitar since he was 16. After 27 years, you'd think he would be better at it. He can also play three chords on a ukulele, and although he hasn't touched a trombone in over 20 years, he is fairly confident that he could still play the Beaver Local Fight Song if he had access to one.

Sam resides in Salem, Ohio, with his wife April and three sons, Brennan, Gavin, and Dashiell.

Justin is a 1994 graduate of LaBrae and did a brief stint at a business school in Pittsburgh that he doesn't really remember the name of. Already a lifelong hockey fan, Justin attended two Phantoms games near the end of the 2010–11 season and a few more the following season. By 2012–13, he had gone all in as a season ticket holder.

His hobbies, outside of supporting the Phantoms, include gaming, anime, and IT. The latter is also his profession, which unfortunately sucks the soul out of him. He's also an avid vodka drinker.

He now lives in Newton Township with his mom, who is also a huge Phantoms fan.

Dan is a 2005 graduate of Austintown Fitch High School. He is president of the United Steelworkers of America Local 2332 and is an overhead craneman at Commercial Metal Forming. He played soccer growing up (when he was in shape and not a shape) and was a three-year letterman in high school. Dan later became the head coach of his alma mater's boys soccer team, where he coached for seven years.

Dan is also an avid drummer, and people who have been to his shows have described his drumming as "average at best."

Dan and his family attended Phantoms games for many years before becoming season ticket holders during the 2021–22 season.

Dan currently resides in Austintown with his wife Heather and sons Gavin and Owen.
